    🖼️ Photos inside the sheet: one-time setup show

    A small helper in your sheet puts every photo inside its cell, like the Excel export. Once a photo is in, it stays there even when this PC is off.

    1. In your Google Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script.
    2. Delete what is in the editor, then paste the helper:
    3. Press 💾 Save. At the top choose setup, then press ▶ Run.
    4. Allow access: Review permissions → your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. It's your own script.

    Photos start appearing within a minute or two, newest first. Keep this PC on until they are in.

    ⚡ Fast mode (recommended)

    Google lets the helper run on its own for only ~90 minutes a day. In fast mode this app starts it for all tabs at once, with no daily limit.
